Label Selectors Describe Sets, Not Partitions

Four Pods carry tier and env labels. Four selectors each resolve independently to their own subset — and those subsets can overlap.

Pod A satisfies three of the four selectors shown here; Pod D satisfies none. A selector describes a set by attribute — it doesn't carve the Pods into exclusive partitions.
