A decision tree drawn as a cascade of yes-or-no diamonds. The first diamond asks whether imagePullPolicy was set explicitly; its YES branch leads to a panel listing the three explicit policies: Always, which resolves to a digest and reuses the cache; IfNotPresent, which pulls only if the image is absent; and Never, which never fetches and fails if the image is absent. The NO branch descends through three more diamonds in turn. Does the reference include a digest? YES ends at IfNotPresent. Was no tag given at all? YES ends at Always. Is the tag :latest? YES ends at Always, drawn with a double outline and annotated as the default nobody chooses on purpose. The final NO, any other tag, ends at IfNotPresent. A legend keys the shapes: decision, outcome, unchosen default, and branch.
