## What this document does and does not say

Five domains, weights 46 / 22 / 16 / 8 / 8. Cloud Native Application Delivery carried 8% under
this blueprint; the current blueprint gives it 16%. This retired document, unlike the current
one, lists sub-topics under each domain: under Application Delivery they are "Application
Delivery Fundamentals", "GitOps" and "CI/CD". Neither "Helm" nor "Kustomize" appears anywhere
in the document. No effective date is printed; the PDF metadata creation date is 2021-09-19.
