# Grafana — Introduction (grafana.com/docs/grafana/latest/introduction/)

Grafana is open source software that enables you to query, visualize, alert on, and explore your metrics, logs, and traces wherever they're stored. Grafana provides tools to turn your time-series database (TSDB) data into insightful graphs and visualizations. Core capabilities: dashboards (turn TSDB data into graphs and visualizations), explore (ad-hoc querying with dynamic drilldown), and alerting (send alerts through channels such as PagerDuty, Slack, and email). Grafana connects to many data sources through its plugin framework, including time-series databases such as Prometheus, SQL and NoSQL databases, log aggregation systems such as Loki, and tracing backends such as Jaeger and Tempo — bringing metrics, logs, and traces into one place.
