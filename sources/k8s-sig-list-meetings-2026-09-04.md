## What this page does not say

everyone. It publishes the schedules and the join links; the openness principle
itself is stated in `k8s-community-governance-2026-08-23` ("work and collaboration
should be done in public"). Chapter prose should therefore say that the roster
publishes each group's meeting schedule, not that every meeting is declared open.
